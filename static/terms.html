Drop

Terms & Refund Policy

Drop is a local rideshare service run by local drivers. You book through this site, pay securely online, and your driver picks you up and drops you at your destination.

The ride

We operate within our posted service area (shown on the homepage). Hours are posted on the homepage — rides requested outside operating hours can't be accepted. Arrival times are estimates; traffic and weather can change them.

Pricing

Fares are quoted upfront before you book: a base fare plus a per-mile charge, shown on the fare calculator. No surge pricing — the quote you see is the fare you pay, plus any tip you choose to add. Tips go 100% to your driver.

Cancellation & refunds

Instant rides: cancel anytime before your driver arrives — from your tracking page — and you get an automatic full refund, no questions asked.

Scheduled rides: cancel free of charge up to 2 hours before pickup for a full refund. Inside 2 hours of pickup, you get a 50% refund — the other half goes to your driver for holding the slot.

If we have to cancel your ride, you also get an automatic full refund. Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually show up in 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Once your ride has started, fares can't be refunded — but if something went wrong with your ride, message us through the support chat on this site and we'll make it right.

Driver conduct

Our drivers are background-checked and their vehicles are inspected. If you ever feel unsafe, end the ride and contact us immediately through the support chat.
