Last updated October 2026 · Drop Services LLC, Bismarck ND

What Drop is

Drop is a local food delivery service run by one driver — Ken. You order from local restaurants through this site, pay securely online, and Ken picks up your food and drops it at your door. Restaurants are not partners of Drop; Ken buys your food at the restaurant like any customer.

Delivery area & hours

We deliver within our posted delivery zone (shown on the homepage). Hours are posted on the homepage — orders placed outside business hours can't be accepted. Delivery times are estimates; traffic, weather, and restaurant wait times can change them.

Prices

Menu prices are set in the app and matched to restaurant menus as closely as possible. A flat delivery fee applies to every order (shown at checkout), plus any tip you choose to add. Tips go 100% to your driver.

Cancellations & refunds

Cancel anytime before Ken picks up your food — from your tracking page — and you get an automatic full refund, no questions asked. If Ken has to cancel your order, you also get an automatic full refund. Refunds go back to your original payment method and usually show up in 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

Once food is picked up from the restaurant, orders can't be cancelled or refunded — but if something's wrong with your order, message Ken through the support chat on this site and he'll make it right.

Promo codes & rewards

Promo codes can't be combined and apply to one order each, while supplies/limits last. Drop Rewards punches are earned on paid, delivered orders and can't be transferred or redeemed for cash.

Your account

You're responsible for keeping your account password private and your delivery address accurate. If Ken can't find you at the address given, the order may be marked delivered after a reasonable wait and attempt to contact you.
