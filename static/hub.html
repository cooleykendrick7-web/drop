📲 Setup (2 minutes):

  1. Download ntfy from App Store (iPhone) or Play Store (Android) — it's free
  2. Open ntfy, tap + to subscribe to a topic
  3. Enter a secret topic name (e.g. drop-driver-yourname-123) — make it unique!
  4. Come back here and enter that same topic name below
  5. Tap Save — you'll now get push alerts instantly

⚠️ Keep your topic secret — anyone with it can send you notifications.
